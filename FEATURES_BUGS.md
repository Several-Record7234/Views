## Feature Ideas

- Import/export.
- Handle scene duplication.
- **Back button.** Snapshot the current viewport before recalling a preset, so a "Back"
  button can return to where you were before the jump.
- **Link presets to Scene objects.** Work out how and why a preset could be tied to an item:
  - Recalling a preset that centres on a hidden image could flip it to visible (a handout reveal).
  - Clicking a designated Scene object could recall a particular preset (a trigger for jumping
    to another part of the Scene).
- **List sorting and organisation.** Options to consider: drag'n'drop reordering, nested
  groups of presets with expand/collapse, and sort by user, name or creation time.
